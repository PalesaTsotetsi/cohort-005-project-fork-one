# Cadence

Cadence sells video courses to learners. This glossary covers the language around getting money back after a purchase, and the learning terms those decisions depend on.

## Language

### People

**Admin**:
A Cadence staff member who runs the platform. Admins are the only people who decide Refund Requests.
_Avoid_: The team, Support, Reviewer

### Refunds

**Refund Request**:
A learner's ask, with their reason, to get back what they paid for a purchase. An Admin approves or declines it. One purchase can have several Refund Requests over time, but only one can be undecided at once. Once decided, the decision is final.
_Avoid_: Refund (for the ask itself), Cancellation, Reversal, Chargeback

**Open Refund Request**:
A Refund Request no Admin has decided yet. The learner keeps their access to the course while it is open.
_Avoid_: Pending, Requested, Awaiting review

**Approved Refund Request**:
A Refund Request an Admin has accepted. Approving it is what leads to a Refund, and it ends the learner's access to the course straight away. Their progress is kept, not deleted, and comes back if they buy the course again.

**Declined Refund Request**:
A Refund Request an Admin has turned down, with a reason the learner is told. It has no Refund, and the learner may raise a new Refund Request.

**Refund Eligibility**:
Whether a learner may raise a Refund Request for a purchase: it must be an individual (not team) purchase, within 14 days, under 50% Course Progress and under 2 hours of Watch Time. It governs asking, not deciding: an Admin may approve a request that no longer meets it.
_Avoid_: Refund policy, Refundable

**Refund**:
The money returned to a learner after their Refund Request is approved. It is always the full amount they paid. A declined Refund Request has no Refund.
_Avoid_: Reversal, Chargeback, Cancellation

**Pending Refund**:
A Refund an Admin has approved where the money hasn't been confirmed as sent yet.
_Avoid_: Pending (for a Refund Request awaiting review)

**Issued Refund**:
A Refund where the money has been confirmed as sent back to the learner.
_Avoid_: Completed, Paid out

**Failed Refund**:
A Refund where the money couldn't be sent. The Refund Request stays approved, because the decision hasn't changed.

### Learning

**Course Progress**:
The share of a course's lessons a learner has completed.
_Avoid_: Progress (for Watch Time), Completion

**Watch Time**:
How much of a course's video a learner has watched, regardless of whether lessons were completed.
_Avoid_: Progress, Time spent
