# Cadence

Cadence sells video courses to students. This glossary pins down the language used in the product, in emails, and in code. Student-facing copy uses these words exactly, in sentence case, with no synonyms.

## Language

### People

**Student**:
A person who buys and takes courses.
_Avoid_: learner, user, customer

**Admin**:
A member of the Cadence team who reviews Refund Requests and decides whether to approve or decline them.
_Avoid_: reviewer, support, "our team"

### Purchases

**Purchase**:
One payment by a student for one course, at a recorded price. A team Purchase is the same thing, except it pays for seats. Buying the same course again creates a new Purchase.
_Avoid_: order, transaction, enrolment

### Refunds

**Refund Request**:
A student's ask to have the money for one Purchase given back, along with their reason. Each Purchase has at most one Refund Request, and a student cannot take it back once it is made.
_Avoid_: Chargeback, Reversal, cancellation, refund (for the request itself)

**Refund**:
The money actually given back to a student, which exists only once a Refund Request is Approved. An approved Refund ends the Purchase, and the student loses access to the course.
_Avoid_: Reversal, chargeback, credit

**Refund Status**:
Where a Refund Request stands: **Pending** (waiting for an Admin), **Approved** (a Refund is given), or **Declined** (nothing is given back, and the decision is final).
_Avoid_: Requested, open, raised (for Pending)

**Refund Amount**:
How much a Refund gives back, which is always exactly what the student paid on that Purchase.
_Avoid_: list price, course price (as the basis for a Refund)

**Refund Window**:
The fourteen calendar days after a Purchase during which a Refund Request may be made. The day of purchase counts as day zero, so the whole of the fourteenth day is included.
_Avoid_: cancel window, cancellation period

**Refund Eligibility**:
Whether a student may make a Refund Request on a Purchase. All of these must hold: they are inside the Refund Window, they have watched less than two hours of the course, they have completed fewer than half its lessons, the Purchase has no Refund Request yet, and no team seat bought with it has been claimed.
_Avoid_: cancellation offer, refundable

**Watched Time**:
How much of a course a student has watched, counted as the furthest point they reached in each lesson and added up across lessons. Skipping ahead counts as watched.
_Avoid_: viewing time, play time

**Chargeback**:
A payment dispute a cardholder raises through their bank, outside Cadence's control. Cadence does not model chargebacks. Never use this word for a Refund Request.
_Avoid_: using it as a synonym for Refund Request or Refund
