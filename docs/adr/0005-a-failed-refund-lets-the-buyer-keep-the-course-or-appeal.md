# A Failed Refund lets the buyer keep the course or appeal

A place on a course (or a Team Licence's Unclaimed Seats) ends at approval, not when the Refund is issued (ADR 0002), and it does not come back on its own if the Refund becomes a Failed Refund. Instead the buyer chooses: keep what the Purchase granted, which gives up the Refund for good, or raise a Refund Appeal to have the money sent again. We chose this over restoring access automatically so a failure never quietly hands back the course while the buyer still expects their money, and over leaving them with nothing, which would be unfair for a failure that wasn't theirs.

## Consequences

- Until the buyer chooses, they have neither the course nor the money. The payout work should prompt them promptly.
- Keeping the course closes the matter: no Refund Appeal later and no new Refund Request on that Purchase.
- A Refund Appeal never reopens the Refund Request, which stays Approved (ADR 0008).
- Nothing here is built yet: the Refund lifecycle (Pending, Issued, Failed) is out of scope until the payout work starts.
